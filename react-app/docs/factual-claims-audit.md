## 1. Audit Framework & Classification Scheme

Every factual proposition across the product detail page (pricing, serving mathematics, ingredient profiles, manufacturing certifications, sensory statements, and biological timelines) is classified under one of three rigorous tiers:

| Tier | Classification Label | Definition & Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **`[VERIFIED: Official Product Information]`** | Directly observable on the live official product page, official retail packaging, or official nutritional specification sheet. |
| **Tier 2** | **`[SUPPORTED: External Scientific Evidence]`** | Validated by peer-reviewed published scientific literature (e.g. PubMed indexed randomized controlled trials or meta-analyses) studying generic collagen peptides. **Explicitly not proven specifically for this exact commercial retail jar.** |
| **Tier 3** | **`[UNVERIFIED: Baseline / Confirmation Required]`** | Unsubstantiated claims, qualitative assumptions, or operational metrics that lack verifiable public documentation and cannot be verified without first-party brand or logistics API access. |

## 3. Summary of Corrections Implemented in Prototype

1. **Strict Review Experience Honesty:** Replaced hardcoded star counts and simulated customer reviews with a transparent, elegant empty state explaining the live webhook connection status.
2. **Methodological Note on Peer-Reviewed Studies:** Added clear visual callouts separating generic bioactive peptide research from finished commercial brand packaging.
3. **Realistic Expectations & Plain-Language Guardrails:** Every benefit chapter explicitly specifies what to realistically expect and what collagen *cannot* do (e.g. not a cure for hair loss or joint disease).
4. **Natural Document Flow:** Removed all sticky/fixed purchase mechanisms so prices and buttons scroll away naturally with the page.
5. **Verified Delivery & Returns Integration:** Integrated the exact official policy across three customer touchpoints: hero purchase panel badge, expandable policy accordion in product specifications, and cart drawer checkout reassurance.
6. **Robust Product Eligibility & Age Reasoning:** Created a dedicated knowledge category for age eligibility, demystifying adult target audience and decline milestones while explicitly disclosing that an exact numerical age is not specified on packaging—eliminating premature fallbacks and never guessing arbitrary limits.
